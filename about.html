About 'Temple World'

By Lewey Geselowitz

Enter an experience of Spiritual Objectivism as a 5-fold KalaCharka temple design, with Zelda inspired mobile touch gameplay.

Also see the Sprial Grid narrative role playing game which is exploring conversational quest mechanics, while this experience is focused on interactive contemplation.

Design

This is an interactive temple meant to express the experience and ideas of Spiritual Objectivism but strongly draws on many lineages, notable the both eastern and western mystical and rational learnings.
